Architecture Summary:
  • Peak Throughput: 16 Multiply-Accumulate operations executed simultaneously on every rising clock edge (2.0 GOPS @ 125 MHz).
  • Skewed Wavefront: Activations are staggered by 1 cycle per row so they meet the accumulating partial sums at the exact right nanosecond at each PE.
  • Output Timing: Final dot-product answers drop out of the South boundary sequentially (Col 0 at T4, Col 1 at T5, Col 2 at T6, Col 3 at T7).
